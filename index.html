Body Composition Strategist: Beyond the Scale

Build a plan that improves health, not just a number on the scale.

You will manage a randomized fictional client through six checkpoints. You will interpret body-composition evidence, choose a realistic goal, build a nutrition and movement strategy, and respond to an eight-week progress update. Several choices may be defensible, so you must select the option that best fits the complete case.

Your goal is not always weight loss. A strong strategy may preserve muscle, reduce waist circumference, improve body-fat percentage, support recovery, or recognize when unexplained weight change requires healthcare evaluation.
Lean tissue Fat tissue Water, bone, organs

Purpose

Use energy-balance and body-composition concepts to distinguish meaningful health changes from scale-only thinking.

Task

Complete six case-specific checkpoints, review the simulated progress data, and answer two short evidence-based questions.

Criteria

Use the assigned client, exact before-and-after values, course concepts, and realistic predictions written in your own thoughts and words.

Instructions

  1. Review the randomized client and the teaching estimates provided.
  2. Complete all six checkpoints. Each checkpoint is worth up to 2 points: 2 points for the strongest case-supported choice, 1 point for a reasonable but incomplete choice, and 0 points for a choice that does not adequately address the evidence.
  3. Use the feedback and the eight-week update to track weight, waist circumference, body-fat percentage, lean tissue, satiety, recovery, sustainability, and health-risk direction.
  4. Answer both short-response questions on the final page.
  5. Select Save Completed Report as PDF, save the PDF to your desktop, and upload it to the Canvas assignment.

Important: All people and outcomes in this activity are fictional. Values are simplified for learning and are not medical advice, a diagnosis, or an individualized calorie prescription.
